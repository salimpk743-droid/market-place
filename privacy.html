Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy applies to the Mobile Market website and the Mobile Market Android app (package pk.mobilemarket.app). The Android app opens this same site in a Trusted Web Activity (Chrome Custom Tabs) — it is not a hidden WebView copy of the site.

What the app and site do

Mobile Market is a classifieds marketplace for used mobile phones in Pakistan. You can search listings, post an ad, edit or mark an ad as sold, and contact sellers.

Information we collect

  • Ads you post: brand, model, price, city, area, PTA status, photos, seller name and phone. On this site those ads are stored in your browser (local storage) on this device.
  • Device storage: your posted ads, sold/deleted flags, and seller unlocks stay on your phone or computer unless you clear site data.
  • We do not create a server account for browsing. Contact details you put on an ad are shown to buyers of that ad.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

When advertising is turned on, Google AdSense may show ads on the website (including inside the Android app, because the app displays the website). Google and its partners use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. This is standard website advertising — not AdSense inside a WebView, which Google does not allow.

You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data for ads.

Permissions (Android)

The Android app requests Internet access only, so it can load listings. It does not read your contacts, location, or files. Photos you attach to an ad are chosen by you in the upload dialog.

Children

Mobile Market is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Delete an ad from My Ads, or clear this site’s data in your browser / Android site settings to remove locally stored listings.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact number on a listing you manage, or the email on the Google Play store listing for Mobile Market.

Consent

By using the website or Android app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
